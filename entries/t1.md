## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Is this a usable research market: does history exist, does it track the underlying instrument, and is trading sufficiently active? This is data feasibility and descriptive analysis, explicitly not a strategy backtest. The report defines research eligibility as confirmed underlying, missing-bar rate ≤2%, at least 30 trading days, recent median daily turnover ≥$10,000, median spread ≤0.20%, every observed spread ≤0.50%, and tracking-error standard deviation ≤1.0%. These classification thresholds are report-defined, not charter rules.

## Data scope

Enumerate Pionex spot instruments rather than hard-code a universe: 32 instruments are identified at the 2026-10-29 19:00 UTC collection. Fetch 60M bars back to each listing and Yahoo adjusted daily closes over 2025-01-01 through 2026-10-30. Tracking comparisons use completed trading days through 2026-10-28; the following partial day is excluded. The longest tracking sample has 287 trading days and the shortest 72. Spreads use 5 snapshots; turnover uses the latest 30 calendar days, including weekends. Derivative-market enumeration is unavailable and outside the conclusion.

## Method

Confirm each underlying using both a price ratio within ±3% on 2026-10-28 and common-day return correlation > 0.9. This is statistical matching, not official mapping documentation. Tracking error is the daily return minus the underlying's adjusted daily return. Estimate turnover by summing hourly volume times close and classify data quality and liquidity separately.

The source uses fixed UTC bars for US closing and premarket prices. Its stated ET equivalences do not cover standard time; the later weekend study corrects that convention. Retain this report's results as stated rather than reinterpret them as corrected estimates. Venue fees were not measured, and the spread snapshots do not represent the full trading day.

## Decisive results

| Classification | Instruments | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Research-feasible | 24 | Data eligibility only, not a trading edge |
| Insufficient liquidity | 6 | Turnover or spread threshold failed |
| Insufficient data quality | 2 | BMNRX and IONQX pricing noise |

All 32 underlying series are statistically matched and hourly histories have missing-bar rate 0.0%, but zero-volume bars account for 9%–42%. BMNRX and IONQX tracking-error standard deviations are 1.23% and 1.24%. CCJX recent median daily turnover is $9,798, below the report's threshold. DXYZX median spread is 0.3640%.

For large weekend deviations, the source's fixed-hour descriptive sample has 293 observations, reversal proportion 53.9%, and mean / median recovery ratio 0.084 / 0.033. These are not strategy returns or a corrected weekend-event estimate.

## Verdict and what was not built

A research-feasible spot subset exists, but this is not a surviving strategy or even WATCH candidacy. Short history, sparse spread sampling, unmeasured fees and unverified official mappings limit the conclusion. Later studies may investigate the eligible subset; noisy instruments require timing checks and marginal liquidity requires monitoring. No strategy, survivor tag or paper bot was produced. No performance comparison with the desk's live system is appropriate.
