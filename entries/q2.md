## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Can the public funding-rate history support research, and do extreme funding rates predict BTC spot returns over 24h and 72h? Stop before building a strategy if either data feasibility or signal existence fails. The prior narrative was that high funding precedes reversal; the report did not assume it was true. Full-history and out-of-sample checks, including stress periods, could not be performed with the available history.

## Data scope

OKX BTC-USDT-SWAP funding rates and BTC-USDT spot 4H bars. Funding history covers 2026-07-27 16:00 UTC to 2026-10-27 16:00 UTC: 277 observations, about 92 days. All timestamps follow the 8h grid, with no gaps or duplicates. Of these, 268 observations have both forward horizons; 9 are right-censored. The last analysis observation is 2026-10-24 16:00 UTC.

## Method

Align funding settlement with the completed spot bar closing at that time, then measure forward close-to-close returns. Compute funding quantiles within the analysis sample. Its lower threshold is +0.000391%; its upper threshold is +0.0100%, also the maximum. Ties make the upper group 36 observations (13.4%), rather than a clean 10%; the lower group contains 27. Compare conditional returns with the unconditional sample and split by month and chronological half.

These are gross spot returns, excluding fees, funding payments, spread and slippage, not strategy returns. Overlapping windows and in-sample thresholds limit inference. The apparent rate ceiling was observed, not verified against exchange rules.

## Decisive results

| Group | Observations | Mean 24h return | 24h positive-return rate | Mean 72h return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unconditional | 268 | +0.28% | 54.1% | +0.83% |
| Lowest funding group | 27 | −0.40% | 48.2% | −0.28% |
| Highest funding group | 36 | +0.70% | 47.2% | +2.21% |

The pooled highest-minus-lowest 72h difference is +2.49 percentage points, opposite to the reversal narrative. Monthly differences are −1.26 percentage points in August, +4.48 in September and +0.60 in October. Of the highest group's 36 observations, 22 fall in August, the strongest baseline month. The highest group's 24h median return is −0.03%. Adjacent 72h windows overlap heavily; nominal sample counts overstate independent evidence.

## Verdict and what was not built

NO-GO: signal existence was not established. The history is too short and the pooled difference does not withstand monthly decomposition. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder. The hypothesis itself is not conclusively disproved: reconsideration requires a longer data source, not repeated analysis of the same rolling window. There is no strategy equity curve or meaningful performance comparison with the desk's live system.
