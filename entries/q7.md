## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

After a market-wide volatility collapse, does volatility continue falling, stay flat, or rebound the next day? Distinguish persistence from mean reversion using ordinary days and a mild-decline placebo. This is descriptive research only; the source specifies event and outcome thresholds but no numerical strategy-promotion threshold.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT; VET is excluded to keep a single venue. Completed 1H bars span 2025-01-01 through 2026-11-08 18:00 UTC. The report states 16,243 continuous bars per asset with no gaps. It lists 676 common complete days through 2026-11-07, 673 evaluable days and 128 events (19.0%). Bar counts are reproduced as stated, not reconciled.

## Method

Daily volatility is the mean absolute 1H return across the UTC day's 24 bars, including the return from the preceding day's final close. An event requires all assets' daily volatility to decline and the median change to be ≤ −25%.

The next-day event-relative ratio is the equal-weight mean of each asset's next-day volatility divided by its event-day volatility. The unconditional-relative ratio uses each asset's full-sample mean daily volatility as denominator. Classify the event-relative ratio as continued decline below 0.95, flat from 0.95 to 1.05, and rebound above 1.05. Compare all eligible days with a placebo of all-asset declines whose median change lies between 0% and −25%. Check individual assets, years and relaxed thresholds. No p-values or strategy performance metrics are reported; costs and volatility-trading instruments are not tested.

## Decisive results

| Group | Days | Continued decline | Flat | Rebound | Event-relative ratio, mean / median | Unconditional-relative ratio, mean / median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Collapse events | 128 | 22.7% | 7.0% | 70.3% | 1.281 / 1.167 | 0.907 / 0.797 |
| All eligible days | 673 | 41.2% | 10.5% | 48.3% | 1.098 / 1.027 | 1.000 / 0.927 |
| Mild-decline placebo | 38 | 39.5% | 10.5% | 50.0% | 1.082 / 1.023 | 1.038 / 1.001 |

Only 32.0% of event days are followed by volatility above the unconditional mean. Decline depth and the next-day event-relative ratio have correlation −0.319: deeper declines precede larger rebounds. Individual-asset rebound proportions range from 65.6% to 71.1%. The source's main table gives an unconditional-relative median of 0.797, while its quantile list gives the corresponding median as 0.801; the discrepancy is unresolved.

## Verdict and what was not built

Descriptively positive, commercially negative: volatility usually rebounds from very low to still below normal. The report attributes this to mean reversion after selecting extreme low-volatility days, not a regime-change signal or a price-direction forecast. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. No filter or add-on to the desk's live system was built.
