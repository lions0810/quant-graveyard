## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Do large underlying moves systematically widen intraday spreads, or is the triggering observation an isolated event? Compare the same instrument across volatility groups rather than confuse instrument composition with widening. Descriptive research only; no numerical strategy-promotion threshold is stated.

## Data scope

Pionex best-bid/ask depth, 60M prices and Yahoo daily prices for all 32 instruments, with 24 research-eligible instruments as the main sample. The panel has 480 instrument-days across 15 weekday snapshots from 2026-10-26 through 2026-11-13, with no missing spreads. Each day has only a midday snapshot; weekends are excluded.

## Method

Divide each spread by its instrument's panel median. Compare underlying full-day absolute returns, instrument morning moves available before the snapshot, and underlying high-low ranges. Groups use <0.5%, 0.5–2% and ≥2%. Full-day conditioning contains post-snapshot information, while morning conditioning is prospective. Use pooled, paired, per-day and per-instrument checks. Quoted spread-crossing costs exclude fees and do not guarantee depth or fills. No strategy comparison with the desk's live system is made.

## Decisive results

| Underlying absolute-return group | Main-group observations | Raw median spread | Normalized median spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet | 50 | 0.0203% | 1.000 |
| Middle | 126 | 0.0212% | 1.000 |
| Large move | 184 | 0.0571% | 1.000 |

The raw gradient is instrument composition. A paired full-day test gives p=0.0225 before correction, but the pre-snapshot morning-condition test gives p=0.7744. Daily normalized medians are 1.249 on 2026-11-05, 1.000 on 2026-11-09 and 1.246 on 2026-11-13: isolated widening is not a stable volatility rule.

## Verdict and what was not built

No systematic widening in the observed midday snapshots. This is not a guarantee about opening, closing or volatility-spike spreads, which the panel cannot observe. Short history, sparse quiet observations and unexplained baseline drift limit generalization. No strategy, WATCH tag, survivor tag or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed.
