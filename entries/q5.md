## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

After all assets rise or all fall on a day, is subsequent 24h or 72h realized volatility above normal? Test the rising and falling groups separately rather than treating breadth as directionless. The source specifies descriptive research only, with no strategy construction and no numerical effect-size kill threshold.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT, using 1H bars from 2025-01-01 through 2026-11-04 18:00 UTC. Each asset has 16,146 continuous bars with no gaps. VET, present in the idea scout's broader observation, is excluded to keep a single venue.

The specification states 668 eligible calendar days, 2025-01-02 through 2026-10-31, with 354 same-direction days (53.0%): 161 all-up and 193 all-down. Elsewhere it names 2026-10-29 as the last evaluable event day; this endpoint discrepancy is unresolved in the source. The triggering 2026-11-04 observation is not evaluable: the day and its forward window were incomplete.

## Method

Use UTC calendar-day close-to-close returns. An event requires all assets to have positive returns or all to have negative returns. From the following day's 00:00 UTC, compute forward realized volatility from summed squared 1H log returns over 24h and 72h. Compare with all eligible days and with event-day volatility. Inspect asset medians, year splits and a relaxed ≥6/7 same-direction definition. Overlapping windows preclude treating days as independent; no p-values are reported. There are no positions, costs deducted or strategy profit-and-loss metrics.

## Decisive results

| Group | Days | Forward 24h / baseline ratio | Forward 72h / baseline ratio | Prior 24h / baseline ratio | Forward / prior 24h |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Same-direction pooled | 354 | 1.055 | 1.023 | 1.078 | 0.978 |
| All-up | 161 | 0.965 | 0.991 | 1.029 | 0.937 |
| All-down | 193 | 1.130 | 1.050 | 1.118 | 1.010 |

All-down forward volatility exceeds baseline across all assets but barely exceeds its already elevated event-day level. All-up days cool afterward. All-down 24h ratios are 1.186 in 2025 and 1.104 in 2026; all-up ratios change from 0.936 to 1.019. The pooled result mixes different directional conditions rather than identifying a common volatility precursor.

## Verdict and what was not built

No systematic volatility expansion after market-wide same-direction days. The all-down effect is persistence of existing high volatility, not a jump; the all-up effect is cooling and is unstable across years. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. No filter or add-on to the desk's live system was built.
