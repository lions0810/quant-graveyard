## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Does overnight earnings pricing systematically overshoot the underlying's actual opening price, and then converge unusually during the session? Anchor the comparison to the underlying open rather than confuse the premarket snapshot with the open. The source specifies descriptive research only, without a numerical effect-size kill threshold.

## Data scope

Pionex 60M bars and Yahoo daily opening and closing prices for 15 earnings-reporting underlyings from the research-eligible universe. Nasdaq calendar scanning covers 2025-09-08 through 2026-11-03, comprising 302 trading dates and 66 calendar events. After unavailable or incomplete observations are excluded, 61 events across 47 reaction days remain through 2026-11-02. Controls contain 3,237 non-earnings weekday nights. BRK-B has no included event because Saturday releases fall outside the weekday scan; this is missing coverage, not a zero effect.

## Method

Nasdaq supplies no release times. Infer the reaction day by choosing the larger absolute underlying opening gap between the report date and the next trading day. Use daylight-saving-corrected 09:00 ET premarket and 16:00 ET closing prices. Let O be the instrument's overnight gap and G the underlying's opening gap; signed overshoot OS is O−G in percentage points. An overshoot requires matching signs and absolute O greater than absolute G. Compare opening recovery, session recovery, years, inferred release timing and instruments. Tight closing tracking mechanically offsets overnight differences during the session, so that offset correlation is not independent evidence. No strategy backtest or executable premarket-price test is performed.

## Decisive results

| Measure | Earnings nights | Non-earnings controls |
|---|---|---|
| Observations | 61 | 3,237 |
| Mean absolute overnight gap | 5.81% | 1.06% |
| Mean absolute underlying opening gap | 5.94% | 1.10% |
| Mean / median signed OS | +0.14pp / +0.18pp | +0.02pp / +0.01pp |
| Overshoot proportion | 42.6% | 40.7% |
| Median opening recovery ratio | −0.015 | +0.030 |
| Median session recovery ratio | −0.009 | +0.056 |

Reaction-day-equal-weight overshoot is 40.6%. The full-day tracking-error standard deviation is 0.21%. The premarket-to-open residual also contains new information arriving between the snapshot and the opening auction, not solely pricing error.

## Verdict and what was not built

No systematic earnings-night overshoot or special session convergence. Opposing anecdotes do not establish a mechanism, and the median event does not recover its overnight gap at the open or during the session. Release-time inference, short history and untested execution limit interpretation. No strategy, survivor tag or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. Performance is not compared with the desk's live system.
