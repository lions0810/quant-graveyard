## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Are weekend quotes active market prices or frozen order remnants, and what spread cost does crossing them imply? Compare weekday and weekend spreads, historical trading activity and intrawindow timing. This is descriptive research only; no numerical strategy-promotion threshold is stated.

## Data scope

All 32 Pionex spot instruments, with the 24 research-eligible instruments as the main group. Data comprise 60M bars through 2026-11-05, a Yahoo trading calendar, and 12 depth snapshots: 10 weekday snapshots and 2 weekend snapshots from a single independent weekend. The historical volume table covers 1,441 instrument-weekends, including 1,113 research-eligible observations, from 2025-09-12 through 2026-10-30. A separate hourly-profile table states 1,193 main-group observations; the source does not reconcile that count.

## Method

Use daylight-saving-corrected Friday 16:00 ET to Monday 09:00 ET windows, excluding holiday Mondays. Separate Friday evening, Saturday, Sunday and Monday premarket activity, and distinguish historical active instruments from the frozen group. Estimate dollar turnover from hourly volume times close. Spread is best ask minus best bid divided by midpoint; quoted crossing cost excludes venue fees and does not guarantee executable depth. Compare the same instruments' weekday behavior and the underlying market, which is closed on weekends. No strategy performance comparison with the desk's live system is made.

## Decisive results

| Snapshot group | Instruments | Median spread amplification | Median weekend spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research-eligible | 24 | 12.07x | 0.6797% |
| Sunday-active | 8 | 1.02x | 0.0042% |
| Sunday-zero-volume | 24 | 20.30x | 1.1368% |

The frozen snapshot group's best bids and asks are identical across both weekend snapshots. Historical research-eligible weekends have Saturday zero-trading proportion 68.3% and median 48 zero-volume bars in the usual 65-hour window. Historical active instruments number 9, including marginal AMZN, rather than the snapshot's 8; the other 15 research-eligible instruments have no historical trades in the stated dead zone. Even the active group loses Saturday trading throughout 2026-05 and 2026-06. The source varies in its exact dead-zone boundary wording; the absence of trading, not a precise resumption hour, is the retained finding.

## Verdict and what was not built

Weekend liquidity is not a stable continuation of weekday liquidity. The report identifies 9 historically active instruments and 23 without a functioning weekend market, while noting that group membership can change. Frozen quotes are not evidence of an executable price; apparent weekend volume may be concentrated in Friday evening and Sunday night instead. No strategy, survivor tag or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed.
