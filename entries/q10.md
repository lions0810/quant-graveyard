## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

After an hourly decline of at least 1.5%, how much of the drop is recovered at subsequent closes over 3 hours? Test recovery proportions, direction, mild-decline controls, decline depth and non-overlapping events. The source specifies descriptive research only and gives no numerical effect-size kill threshold.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT, using completed 1H bars. The report states 16,387 continuous bars per asset with no gaps through 2026-11-14 18:00 UTC. There are 114,681 eligible asset-hours and 2,915 events from 2025-01-01 01:00 through 2026-11-14 15:00 UTC. Events require the preceding bar and the complete forward window; incomplete outcomes are excluded.

## Method

An event has hourly close-to-close return ≤ −1.5%. Divide the change from the event close to each subsequent close by the price drop from the preceding close to the event close. R3 is the recovery ratio at the 3h close; the maximum across forward closes is a separate, retrospectively selected statistic, not a realizable exit rule. Compare unconditional forward returns, declines between −1.5% and −0.75%, depth groups, years and assets. A greedy non-overlapping subset requires event spacing ≥4h. No p-values are reported because events cluster. This is not a strategy equity curve or a next-open execution test.

## Decisive results

| Group | Events | R3 mean | R3 median | Any recovery | Recovery ≥0.5 | Full recovery ≥1.0 | Mean forward 3h return |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All sharp declines | 2,915 | 0.041 | 0.080 | 54.8% | 28.2% | 12.7% | +0.10% |
| Non-overlapping | 2,341 | 0.021 | 0.067 | 54.0% | 27.3% | 12.0% | +0.05% |

Continued declines occur in 45.0% of all events. The unconditional mean forward return is −0.01%. The mild-decline placebo has mean forward return +0.01%, despite higher proportions crossing fractional-recovery thresholds because its denominator is smaller. Recovery ratios do not increase monotonically with decline depth. BTC and ETH mean R3 are −0.018 and −0.064. Yearly median R3 values are 0.079 in 2025 and 0.082 in 2026.

## Verdict and what was not built

No systematic recovery of meaningful size. The full-event mean forward return equals the stated round-trip fee of 0.10%, before spread and slippage; the non-overlapping mean is lower. A large rebound in a later individual bar is not the same as recovery over the full window. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. No performance comparison with the desk's live system is appropriate.
