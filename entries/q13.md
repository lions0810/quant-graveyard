## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

After a large rise in the 14:00 UTC bar, does the 15:00 bar systematically give it back? An event requires the panel's median 14:00 return to be ≥ +1.0%. Compare unconditional hours, mild-rise and decline placebos, years, assets, rise-size groups and weekdays versus weekends. The source specifies descriptive research only and a cost-size check against a 0.10% round-trip fee, not a numerical strategy-promotion threshold.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT; VET is excluded. The report states 16,530 continuous 1H bars per asset through 2026-11-20 17:00 UTC, with no gaps. There are 689 UTC days from 2025-01-01 through 2026-11-20, each containing both relevant bars, and 67 events.

## Method

The source explicitly defines the 14:00 bar as 14:00–15:00 UTC and the 15:00 bar as 15:00–16:00 UTC. These are fixed UTC windows, not a daylight-saving-adjusted equity opening window; the source's later clock-conversion wording is inconsistent. Compute equal-weight panel returns for each bar, then the giveback ratio G as the negative next-bar return divided by the first-bar return. A ratio of 1.0 is full giveback; a negative ratio is continuation. Check single-asset events without optimizing asset-specific rules. No positions or strategy equity curve are constructed.

## Decisive results

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Events | 67 |
| Mean / median 14:00 return | +1.67% / +1.42% |
| Mean / median 15:00 return | +0.10% / −0.09% |
| Negative next-bar return | 50.7% |
| Mean / median G | 0.011 / 0.037 |
| Full giveback | 7.5% |
| Continued rise | 49.3% |

The mild-rise placebo's mean next-bar return is −0.03%, versus −0.00% for both declining first bars and unconditional days. Event-group mean next-bar return changes from −0.02% in 2025 to +0.34% in 2026. The largest rise group, ≥2.0%, has next-bar mean +0.68%, not stronger reversal. Weekend events number only 5, preventing a reliable weekend comparison.

## Verdict and what was not built

No systematic giveback: direction is near evenly split, the mean and median have opposite signs, and year splits reverse direction. Higher thresholds do not rescue the reversal hypothesis. The source's illustrative fee comparison leaves neither shorting for reversal nor buying for continuation with a tradable margin. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. No performance comparison with the desk's live system is appropriate.
