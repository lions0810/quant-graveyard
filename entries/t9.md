## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Does a larger weekday overnight/session volume ratio predict a larger opening gap or subsequent session volatility? Separate simultaneous gap association from advance prediction. Descriptive research only; no numerical predictive-effect kill threshold is stated.

## Data scope

Pionex 60M bars and Yahoo daily prices for 24 research-eligible instruments, split into 9 weekend-active instruments and 15 frozen-market controls. The stated overnight sample spans 2025-09-08 through 2026-11-12: 4,553 instrument-nights, including 2,115 active-group observations. Only adjacent calendar-day trading pairs qualify; weekends and holiday gaps are excluded. The latest overnight contributes premarket outcomes but no incomplete session outcomes.

## Method

Use daylight-saving-corrected ET times. Overnight volume runs from 16:00 to 09:00, divided by prior session dollar turnover. Separate volume available by 06:00 from the last 3 hours. The reported opening gap ends at 09:00 premarket, not the equity open. Session outcomes run to 16:00; the range proxy uses hourly closes, not intrabar highs and lows. Compare thirds, years, instruments and within-instrument demeaned relationships. No strategy returns or cost-adjusted execution are tested; underlying daily returns provide context, not comparison with the desk's live system.

## Decisive results

The full overnight ratio has a high-minus-low mean absolute gap difference of +0.417pp. Last-window volume versus absolute gap has Pearson correlation 0.212, stronger than the early ratio's 0.122. Early volume's within-instrument Spearman correlation is only 0.033 (p≈0.13). The full ratio versus subsequent session range has Pearson −0.009 and Spearman 0.002 over 2,106 observations. The apparent gap association is concentrated near simultaneous price discovery; it does not translate into reliable next-session volatility prediction.

## Verdict and what was not built

Descriptive gap association is positive, but the advance predictive claim is negative, as is subsequent session volatility prediction. This does not contradict the separate weekend early-volume convergence pattern. No strategy, WATCH tag, survivor tag or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed.
