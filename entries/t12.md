## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Is the historically active group's Saturday-morning trading nearly absent compared with the later weekend window? Determine whether thin activity is persistent or regime-dependent. Descriptive research only; no numerical strategy-promotion threshold is stated.

## Data scope

Pionex 60M bars for AAPLX, AMZNX, CRCLX, GOOGLX, METAX, NVDAX, QQQX, SPYX and TSLAX. The sample states 62 Friday-labeled weekends from 2025-09-12 through 2026-11-13, totaling 558 instrument-weekends, with 10,455 hourly bars per instrument and no missing bars. Partial listing and forming bars are excluded or replaced with completed values.

## Method

Saturday morning consists of bars opening at 09:00, 10:00 and 11:00 ET. The comparison window, named Sunday night in the source, actually runs from Sunday 12:00 through Monday 09:00 ET and contains 21 bars. Use daylight-saving-corrected windows, dollar turnover estimated from volume times close, zero-volume proportions, active-instrument counts and retrospective regime splits. No strategy returns, trading costs or performance comparison with the desk's live system are reported.

## Decisive results

Saturday-morning median turnover is $17,072 versus $110,411 in the comparison window; median volume share is 3.09% and median window-to-window ratio 0.109. Zero-volume proportions are 29.4% versus 2.0%. The earlier regime has 32 weekends with all instruments trading; a subsequent 14-weekend block has exactly zero Saturday-morning volume for the entire group. Activity then partially recovers, while AMZN remains absent. The latest event weekend contains unusually large activity, contradicting any permanent no-market assumption. News attribution is a possible explanation, not a causal test.

## Verdict and what was not built

Near-absence is a qualified median description, not a full-history constant. Regime blocks and instrument-specific absences must be accounted for before using weekend volume conditions. Sudden activity can flag an event for investigation, not a trading signal. No strategy, WATCH tag, survivor tag or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed.
