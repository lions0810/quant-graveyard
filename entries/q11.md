## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Do particular UTC hours have systematically positive or negative average hourly returns? Check direction separately from volatility. This is descriptive research only. The source applies a Bonferroni screen across 24 hours at family error rate 5%, with an approximate absolute t threshold of 2.87, and compares effect size with fees. Statistical significance alone does not establish tradability.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT. The report states 16,434 continuous 1H bars per asset with no gaps through 2026-11-16 17:00 UTC, yielding 115,031 hourly returns from 2025-01-01 01:00 UTC. Its hour labels are explicitly described as closing-hour labels, although later reports describe their supposedly shared convention differently; results below retain this report's labels.

## Method

Pool asset returns by UTC hour and report signed means, medians, positive-return proportions and t-statistics. Compare all hours, weekdays versus weekends, years and individual assets; winsorize each asset at 1% and 99% as an outlier check. Do not optimize hours separately by asset. Hourly clustering can inflate nominal t-statistics, so the screen is not the primary evidence. Compare gross hourly means with the stated round-trip fee of 10bp; no strategy or equity curve is constructed.

## Decisive results

| Reported UTC hour | Mean return, bp | Median return, bp | Positive-return proportion | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08:00 | +3.24 | +1.34 | 50.7% | +3.35 |
| 06:00 | −4.44 | −0.26 | 48.8% | −4.64 |
| 13:00 | −4.99 | −3.87 | 46.7% | −4.00 |
| 14:00 | +0.93 | +3.10 | 51.5% | +0.64 |

Across the full hourly table, means range from −4.99 to +3.24bp and positive-return proportions from 46.7% to 52.7%. The positive screened hour, 08:00, changes from +5.13bp on weekdays to −1.47bp on weekends. The strongest positive hours in 2025 turn negative in 2026. Some negative hours retain their signs across splits, but their means remain smaller than the round-trip fee.

## Verdict and what was not built

No tradable directional clock. Volatility timing does not imply return direction, and nominally significant small means do not cover costs or survive all splits. The source's illustrative short-side fee totals are internally inconsistent; they are not reproduced here. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. No performance comparison with the desk's live system is appropriate.
