## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

After a market-wide all-up or all-down day, does the next day continue, reverse completely, or diverge across assets? Compare conditional direction with ordinary days. This is descriptive research, not a strategy candidate; the report does not specify a numerical effect-size kill threshold.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT. VET is excluded to retain a single venue. Completed 1H bars span 2025-01-01 through 2026-11-06 18:00 UTC, with 16,194 continuous bars per asset and no gaps. There are 674 common complete days through 2026-11-05 and 672 evaluable days. Events total 356 (53.0%): 162 all-up and 194 all-down.

## Method

Define event direction using UTC calendar-day close-to-close returns, requiring every asset to rise or every asset to fall. Continuation means every asset has the same direction the following day; complete reversal means every asset reverses; all other outcomes are divergence. Measure individual-asset continuation and next-day equal-weight gross returns.

Compare with all 672 eligible days using each day's majority direction, and with 316 non-event days. Check medians, individual assets, years, ≥6/7 alignment, and consecutive versus isolated events. Dependence prevents independent-sample significance claims; no p-values are reported. No positions or strategy equity curve are constructed; fees, spreads and executable next-open gaps are not assessed.

## Decisive results

| Event group | Days | Continuation | Complete reversal | Divergence | Individual-asset continuation | Next-day equal-weight return, mean / median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pooled | 356 | 27.5% | 27.2% | 45.2% | 50.4% | −0.07% / +0.06% |
| All-up | 162 | 24.1% | 27.8% | 48.1% | 49.1% | +0.05% / +0.07% |
| All-down | 194 | 30.4% | 26.8% | 42.8% | 51.4% | −0.17% / +0.06% |

All eligible days have continuation of 27.4% and complete reversal of 25.7%; non-event days have 27.2% and 24.1%. Event-day continuation therefore adds no material information. For all-up events, continuation versus reversal changes from 26.2% versus 21.4% in 2025 to 21.8% versus 34.6% in 2026. The apparent preferred direction is not stable.

## Verdict and what was not built

No systematic next-day continuation or reversal at a tradable scale. This is not proof that the effect is exactly zero. An exploratory consecutive-all-up anomaly lacked a pre-registered directional hypothesis and would require new out-of-sample data, not further slicing of this sample. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. No filter or add-on to the desk's live system was built.
