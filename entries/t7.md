## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Does weekend trading concentration identify larger Monday gaps or different subsequent convergence? Separate genuinely active weekend instruments from frozen-market controls. This is descriptive research only; no numerical predictive-effect kill threshold is stated. The within-instrument relationship is the key check against cross-instrument composition effects.

## Data scope

Pionex 60M bars and Yahoo adjusted daily prices for the 24 research-eligible instruments. The main group is AAPL, AMZN, CRCL, GOOGL, META, NVDA, QQQ, SPY and TSLA; the other 15 instruments are frozen-market controls. Complete weekends from 2025-09-12 through 2026-10-30 supply 1,059 instrument-weekends: 486 active and 573 frozen. The unfinished current weekend is excluded.

## Method

Use daylight-saving-corrected ET windows. Divide total Friday-close-to-Monday-premarket dollar turnover by Friday session turnover; separately use Sunday-night turnover. Compare pooled volume thirds, demean within instrument, inspect years and individual instruments, and remove unusually large ratios as a denominator check. The reported Monday gap G ends at 09:00 ET, not the actual equity open; session return then runs to 16:00. Convergence statistics use absolute G ≥0.5%. Total weekend volume and G complete simultaneously, so their association cannot establish an advance signal. No strategy or full trading costs are tested; underlying Monday returns provide context, not comparison with the desk's live system.

## Decisive results

| Main-group total-volume third | Observations | Mean absolute G | Median absolute G | Convergence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 162 | 0.988% | 0.876% | 40.0% |
| Middle | 162 | 0.986% | 0.677% | 55.2% |
| High | 162 | 1.140% | 0.653% | 55.6% |

Pooled volume versus absolute G has Pearson correlation 0.047 and Spearman −0.054. Within-instrument Spearman is −0.175, opposite to the proposed positive relationship. The total-volume high-minus-low convergence difference is +15.6pp, but changes to −1.4pp under the Sunday-night definition and is unstable across years. Frozen-market controls realize most volume in the same window as their price movement; that is contemporaneous trading, not prediction.

## Verdict and what was not built

No reliable prediction of Monday gap magnitude or convergence from weekend volume. Cross-instrument composition, ratio denominators and simultaneous measurement undermine apparent relationships. The weak negative within-instrument association is not established as a reverse trading signal. No strategy, WATCH tag, survivor tag or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed.
