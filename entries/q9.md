## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Is crypto volatility systematically higher around the US equity opening hour and during its trading session? Compare fixed UTC hours and daylight-saving-adjusted groups, with weekends as a placebo. This is descriptive research only, not a strategy candidate; no numerical effect-size kill threshold is stated.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT; VET is excluded because it is unavailable on the venue. The report states 16,338 continuous completed 1H bars per asset through 2026-11-12 17:00 UTC, with no gaps. The analysis uses 679 complete UTC days, 2025-01-02 through 2026-11-11: 485 weekdays and 194 weekend days. The initial day lacks the preceding close; the final partial day is excluded.

## Method

Measure each hour's absolute close-to-close return, normalize by that asset's full-sample average absolute hourly return, then equally weight assets. Also inspect raw means, medians and the frequency of each hour containing the day's largest absolute move or decline. Identify the opening bar as 13:00 UTC during EDT and 14:00 during EST; compare it with other session and off-session bars. Check individual assets and years. Volatility clustering prevents treating all bars as independent; no p-values or strategy performance metrics are reported. Costs and price-direction predictability are not tested.

## Decisive results

| Subset | Days | 14:00 volatility ratio | 15:00 volatility ratio | 13:00 volatility ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All complete days | 679 | 1.485 | 1.439 | 1.239 |
| Weekdays | 485 | 1.707 | 1.643 | 1.365 |
| Weekends | 194 | 0.93 | 0.93 | 0.92 |

The daylight-saving-adjusted opening-bar mean ratio is 1.354, versus 1.153 for other session bars and 0.911 off-session. The 14:00 hour ranks highest in 2025 and 2026, with ratios 1.64 and 1.30. It ranks highest for most assets and next-highest for the remainder. The weekday peak disappears on weekends.

## Verdict and what was not built

The opening-hour volatility pattern is descriptively supported, but tradability is unverified. High absolute movement does not predict profitable direction, and the distribution of the day's largest declines is retrospective, not an entry signal. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. No performance comparison with the desk's live system is appropriate.
