## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Does expanding volume with little net price movement precede higher realized volatility over 24h or 72h? Separate volatility already present during the event from subsequent volatility. This is descriptive research, explicitly not a strategy candidate. The source registers event definitions but gives no numerical pass/fail threshold for the effect.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT; VET is excluded because it is unavailable on that venue. Completed 1H bars cover 2025-01-01 00:00 UTC through 2026-10-30 18:00 UTC, with 16,027 bars per asset and no gaps. Eligible event windows run from 2025-02-01 to 2026-10-27. The baseline contains 106,484 overlapping bar-level observations, not independent samples.

## Method

The primary event requires trailing 24h quote turnover divided by preceding 24h turnover ≥ 1.8, with absolute 24h net return < 1%. Accept events chronologically and skip the next 72 bars after each accepted event. Measure realized volatility as the square root of summed squared hourly log returns, separately over the forward 24h and 72h and the prior 24h. Compare events with all eligible bars and inspect medians, individual assets and years; do not report p-values because observations are dependent.

Sensitivity definitions use volume relative to the median of the past 30 non-overlapping days, or raise the primary volume-ratio threshold to ≥ 2.5. There are no positions or profit-and-loss series; strategy performance metrics do not apply.

Volatility levels in the table are percentages; parenthesized values are ratios. Primary forward 24h volatility is below baseline in every asset except SOL. The yearly forward 24h ratios are 0.93 in 2025 and 0.90 in 2026. The median-volume definition selects an already volatile regime: subsequent volatility remains above baseline but falls from its prior level.

## Verdict and what was not built

Volume expansion with flat prices did not forecast a volatility increase. The observed volume expansion coincided with elevated current volatility, followed by cooling. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. Descriptive volatility measurements are not a performance comparison with the desk's live system.
