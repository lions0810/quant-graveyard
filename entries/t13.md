## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Does late-weekend direction agree with the underlying's Monday opening gap, and does it outperform the whole-weekend deviation? Compare size thresholds, conflicting early/late signs and stability. The source pre-registers unadjusted opening and preceding closing prices for the gap denominator. Descriptive research only; no numerical strategy-promotion threshold is stated.

## Data scope

Pionex 60M bars and Yahoo daily open, close and adjusted close for 24 research-eligible instruments, split into 9 historically active instruments and 15 frozen controls; leveraged underlyings are separately checked. The sample states 1,098 instrument-weekends across 55 Mondays from 2025-09-12 through 2026-11-13. Nonzero-direction comparisons use 1,050 observations. Partial initial weekends and holiday Mondays are excluded; price anchors are checked against newly completed data.

## Method

Use daylight-saving-corrected ET closing timestamps. Late deviation runs from Sunday 20:00 to Monday 09:00, not the earlier study's Sunday-noon window. Compare its sign and magnitude with the underlying's actual 09:30 opening gap, calculated from unadjusted prices. Compare full-weekend and earlier deviations, conflicting signs, years, instruments and Monday-level clustering. High agreement can reflect the same price-discovery process observed shortly apart, rather than an executable edge. No strategy or comparison with the desk's live system is constructed.

## Decisive results

Late-window/opening-gap directional agreement is 86.8%, rising to 98.5% for absolute late moves ≥2%. Whole-weekend agreement is higher at 91.5%; early-window agreement is 59.4%. When late and full-weekend signs disagree, the open follows the full weekend in 62 of 78 cases and the late window in 16. Monday-level median agreement is 86.7%. Median late contribution to weekend absolute log movement is 0.797. These are tracking statistics, not strategy win rates or profits.

## Verdict and what was not built

Directional agreement is supported, but the late window has no independent forecasting status beyond the full weekend. By the premarket observation much of the opening gap is already priced; direction agreement does not establish a tradable residual or forecast subsequent session returns. No strategy, WATCH tag, survivor tag or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed.
