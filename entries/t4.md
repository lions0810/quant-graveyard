## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

After a large weekday overnight deviation, does the next session reverse or continue it? Does the absolute overnight threshold ≥ 2% add information beyond ordinary nights? This is descriptive research only, with no numerical predictive-effect kill threshold stated.

## Data scope

The 24 research-eligible Pionex instruments, including 4 leveraged-ETF underlyings; 60M bars and Yahoo trading dates and daily prices through the last complete session, 2026-10-30. There are 4,628 weekday overnight pairs and 813 large deviations across 217 subsequent sessions. Friday-to-Monday pairs are excluded. Holiday-spanning pairs are separately identified; the large-deviation sample includes 26 of them. The current incomplete Monday is not an outcome observation.

## Method

Use daylight-saving-corrected ET times. Overnight O runs from the preceding 16:00 close to the next 09:00 premarket close; session return I runs from that price to 16:00. Recovery ratio R is −I/O. Compare all nights, signs, size groups, years, weekdays, leverage and individual instruments. Equally weight subsequent sessions to address clustering. Premarket closes are snapshots, not guaranteed executable opening prices; no strategy or full cost-adjusted equity curve is constructed. Underlying daily returns provide the tracking comparison, not the desk's live system.

## Decisive results

| Group | Observations | Reversal | Continuation | Median R | Recovery ≥ half | Full recovery |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All weekday nights | 4,628 | 48.8% | 48.0% | 0.02 | 39.2% | 30.7% |
| Large deviations | 813 | 48.6% | 51.2% | −0.02 | 26.2% | 11.6% |

The large-deviation session mean / median return is +0.00% / +0.04%, while mean absolute return is 2.65%: near-zero signed movement does not mean quiet sessions. Session-equal-weight reversal is 49.7%. Reversal changes from 58.9% in 2025 to 47.0% in 2026. Individual instruments split between opposing directions. Full-day tracking-error standard deviation is 0.17%.

## Verdict and what was not built

No predictive weekday overnight reversal. The threshold selects larger volatility, not directional information; yearly splits reverse and weighting does not reveal an edge. Differences between premarket and opening-price definitions also alter anecdotal recovery amounts. No strategy, survivor tag or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed.
