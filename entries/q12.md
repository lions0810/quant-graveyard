## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

When every asset's worst hour of the UTC day coincides and the median hourly return is ≤ −1.5%, is the subsequent 6h recovery systematic? Compare ordinary hours, large declines without synchronized daily-worst labels, and synchronized worst hours below the decline threshold. This is descriptive research only; no numerical strategy-promotion threshold is stated. The implementability audit was added afterward, not pre-registered.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT. The report states 16,482 continuous 1H bars per asset through 2026-11-18 17:00 UTC, with no gaps; 16,481 common hourly returns begin at 2025-01-01 01:00 UTC. It lists 687 UTC days, 91 events, and 16,475 unconditional forward observations. VET is outside the panel. The source describes its hour labels as closing hours; these labels are retained without reconciling the convention with later reports.

## Method

Identify each asset's worst hourly return over the complete UTC day. Require every asset's worst hour to coincide and the median return to cross the registered decline threshold. Compute equal-weight forward 6h returns and asset-level price-recovery ratios, summarized as R6. A ratio of 1.0 means full recovery, and a negative ratio means continued decline. Check years and individual assets.

Crucially, the daily-worst label is unavailable at the event close. The later audit therefore tests all threshold-crossing declines without the daily-worst condition, and separately measures the remaining window after day-end confirmation. Neither audit is a strategy backtest or next-open execution test.

## Decisive results

| Group | Observations | Median R6 | Mean forward 6h return | Median forward 6h return | Positive-return proportion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Synchronized worst-hour events | 91 | 0.242 | +0.64% | +0.63% | 69.2% |
| Large-decline placebo | 245 | −0.038 | −0.11% | −0.09% | 47.8% |
| Unconditional hours | 16,475 | — | −0.02% | +0.02% | 50.7% |
| Synchronized worst hours below decline threshold | 50 | 0.605 | — | +0.66% | — |

Full recovery occurs in 9.9% of events; 31.9% continue declining. Removing the retrospective label leaves 336 large-decline hours with mean forward return +0.095%, approximately zero after the stated round-trip fee of 0.10%. Waiting for day-end confirmation leaves only 15 residual-window observations, with mean return +0.34% and stated after-fee return +0.24%; this is a small, post-hoc subset.

## Verdict and what was not built

Descriptively positive, trading negative. The apparent rebound depends on a label requiring the entire day's data. Controls support a retrospective selection explanation, not proof of a mechanical rebound law; the source acknowledges it cannot conclusively distinguish the mechanisms. The version without future information has no meaningful margin after fees, and the day-end subset is insufficient. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. No performance comparison with the desk's live system is appropriate.
