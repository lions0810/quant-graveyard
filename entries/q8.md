## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

After a market-wide volatility spike, does next-day volatility remain elevated, stay flat, or fall? Compare ordinary days and a mild-rise placebo to distinguish a spike-specific effect from general daily mean reversion. This is descriptive research, not a strategy candidate; there is no numerical strategy-promotion threshold in the source.

## Data scope

OKX spot BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, DOGE and XRP against USDT; VET is excluded to retain a single venue. Completed 1H bars span 2025-01-01 through 2026-11-10 18:00 UTC. The report states 16,290 continuous bars per asset with no gaps. There are 678 common complete days through 2026-11-09, 675 evaluable days and 29 events (4.3%). The triggering 2026-11-09 observation is a mild-rise placebo, not a primary event, and its next day was incomplete at collection.

## Method

Daily volatility is the mean absolute 1H return across the UTC day's 24 bars, including the return from the preceding day's final close. Events require every asset's volatility to rise and the median increase to be ≥ +100%.

Compute an equal-weight mean of asset-level next-day/event-day volatility ratios, and a separate ratio against each asset's full-sample unconditional mean. Classify the event-relative ratio as cooling below 0.95, flat from 0.95 to 1.05, or continued elevation above 1.05. The mild-rise placebo requires every asset's volatility to rise but the median increase to lie between 0% and +100%. Inspect individual assets, years, medians, a ≥50% threshold and ≥6/7 rising assets. No p-values or strategy performance metrics are reported. No costs or volatility-trading instruments are tested.

## Decisive results

| Group | Days | Cooling | Flat | Continued elevation | Event-relative ratio, mean / median | Unconditional-relative ratio, mean / median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spike events | 29 | 62.1% | 6.9% | 31.0% | 0.908 / 0.735 | 1.262 / 0.934 |
| All eligible days | 675 | 41.0% | 10.5% | 48.4% | 1.100 / 1.027 | 1.000 / 0.927 |
| Mild-rise placebo | 137 | 56.2% | 12.4% | 31.4% | 0.964 / 0.887 | 1.096 / 1.010 |

Spike depth and the next-day event-relative ratio have correlation +0.143: deeper spikes do not yield systematically stronger cooling. Only 48.3% of events have next-day volatility above the unconditional mean. The event-group unconditional-relative median changes from 1.412 in 2025 to 0.797 in 2026, so the pooled near-average endpoint hides regime differences. Continued elevation remains a substantial tail at 31.0%.

## Verdict and what was not built

Descriptively positive, commercially negative: cooling is common but generally returns volatility to its unconditional mean, and mild rises also cool. The small event sample and year-dependent endpoint limit inference. This is not a price-direction forecast or evidence that shorting volatility is profitable. No strategy, survivor tag, paper bot or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed. No filter or add-on to the desk's live system was built.
