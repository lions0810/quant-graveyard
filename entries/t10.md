## Pre-registered question and kill criteria

Does the premium systematically widen or contract on large-move underlying days? Distinguish signed level from dispersion and control shared premium regimes. The source specifies descriptive research only and no numerical strategy-promotion threshold.

## Data scope

Pionex 60M bars and Yahoo daily and hourly prices for 24 research-eligible instruments, including 4 leveraged-ETF underlyings. The closing sample spans 2025-09-08 through 2026-11-10: 5,909 instrument-days across 302 trading days. The intraday sample has 5,839 observations. Non-leveraged and liquid subsets provide sensitivity checks; incomplete latest daily prices are excluded.

## Method

Closing premium Pc compares the 16:00 ET instrument close with the unadjusted underlying close. Compare absolute underlying returns ≥2% with <1%, subtract daily panel median Pc, and check instrument-level 20-day rolling medians and within-regime groups. Mann–Whitney and Spearman tests are descriptive, without multiple-comparison correction. The intraday comparison pairs 13:00 ET instrument prices with 13:30 underlying prices; its timing noise prevents small-effect inference. No strategy or arbitrage feasibility is tested, and performance is not compared with the desk's live system.

## Decisive results

The main daily-demeaned premium-level test gives MWU p=0.895 and Spearman +0.005. Raw absolute-premium medians rise from 0.0316% in the <1% return group to 0.0797% in the ≥5% group, but dispersion is not signed premium expansion. Demeaned absolute deviations remain larger by about +0.015pp. Last-hour price movement versus absolute premium residual has Spearman +0.200; timing mismatch can explain part, not all, of the dispersion. Intraday zero results are below the comparison's resolution, not independent proof of absence.

## Verdict and what was not built

No systematic premium expansion or contraction after regime control. Greater dispersion is a noisy-closing-price phenomenon at spread-scale magnitude, not a demonstrated tradable premium effect. Daily demeaning could remove a genuinely shared effect; residual timing mechanisms remain unresolved. No strategy, WATCH tag, survivor tag or handoff to the strategy builder; the line is closed.
